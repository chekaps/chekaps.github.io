About Me

I am a Computer Engineering student at Texas A&M University with a strong interest in digital hardware, RTL design, hardware verification, computer architecture, and advanced computing systems.

Before beginning my formal digital design coursework, I independently started learning digital logic and Verilog and built projects such as adders and an 8-bit ALU. That early preparation helped me develop hands-on experience with testbenches, simulation, waveform analysis, and digital hardware debugging.

My broader engineering experience also includes Python, NVIDIA Jetson hardware, Arduino-based projects, robotics, and hardware/software integration. My goal is to continue growing as a hardware engineer and contribute to the development of next-generation computing systems.
